What this is

A museum, not a scanner.

Products die in blog posts. Their sockets often do not. This hall hangs a small number of real probes next to the obituaries that were supposed to end them.

Still answering means HTTP 200 on a surface the owner declared dead or sold. Auth ghost means the host still speaks the old protocol — 401, not 410. Successor façade means the old name redirects into someone else’s living product. Buried means they actually returned 410. Parse is here so the hall is honest: some funerals finish.

We do not publish exploit paths, tokens, or “how to keep calling this.” Every frame is stamped DO NOT INTEGRATE. Probes are GET/HEAD on public URLs with a museum user-agent.

Ghost Museum is its own project. It is not an addon to Chrysalis, WISPTools, Helix, or the Fragility Discovery Engine.
