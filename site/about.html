Companies publish sunsets. Visitors assume the thing is gone. Often the hostname still answers, the API still speaks 401 instead of 410, or the old name opens someone else’s living product. That incomplete funeral is what hangs here.

How frames get hung

Both. People may nominate a public URL and its obituary. We also hunt — reading sunsets and probing the sockets they left behind.

Nothing hangs on rumor. A frame needs a live probe (or an honest unprobed / failed label) and a cited funeral. The museum decides what goes on the wall. The stamp is always the same: DO NOT INTEGRATE.

Nominate

Send a public probe URL + an obituary link. No account. No tokens. No “how to keep calling it.” We probe; if it belongs, it hangs.

Open the nomination form — curator gate, bot traps, never auto-published.

House rules

  • GET only on public URLs. Museum user-agent. No auth bypass, fuzzing, or load tests.
  • No exploit paths, credentials, or integration guides — ever.
  • Small hall. A museum, not a directory of every dead hostname on earth.
  • Own project. Not Chrysalis, WISPTools, Helix, or the Fragility Discovery Engine.
